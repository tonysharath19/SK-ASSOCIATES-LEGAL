UPDATES & GUIDES

Read before you meet.

Use these guides to prepare for a conversation. Choose a topic to open its preparation checklist.

Read the date and jurisdiction first.

When reviewing a notice or announcement, identify the issuing authority, state or Union Territory and effective date. Keep the original alongside your notes so you can discuss the wording during your consultation.

Key terms

Issuing authority

The body that sent the notice or made the decision.

Jurisdiction

The state, Union Territory or central authority whose rules apply to your matter.

Effective date

The date a rule, notice or decision takes effect.

Response date

The date by which something must be done or sent.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you enquire.

What if I have several issues?

Select the service closest to the immediate decision and briefly mention the related issues in your enquiry.

What should I leave out of the form?

Use a general description. Keep identification numbers, medical records and private documents for an agreed method of exchange.

How do I ask about cost?

Ask which work is included, how fees are calculated and what expenses may arise. Request written terms before proceeding.

Does this site store my details?

No. The form prepares an email draft that you review and send yourself.

Which locations can I ask about?

Mention your state or Union Territory in the enquiry so the team can discuss the support available where you live.
